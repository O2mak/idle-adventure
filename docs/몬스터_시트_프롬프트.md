쓰는 법: 제미나이 앱에 **한국 몬스터 3장**(`몬스터_참고그림`의 도깨비, 구미호, 도깨비대장)과 고양이 그림을 먼저 첨부 → 아래 공통 지시문 → 맨 끝 "Country:" 뒤에 나라 줄을 붙여 넣기. 나라당 한 번씩, 총 9번. 마음에 안 들면 같은 대화에서 "몬스터 2번만 다시"처럼 요청.
받은 그림은 `바탕화면\몬스터_참고그림`에 `2지역_일본.png`처럼 저장 (파일 이름 앞의 숫자는 지역 번호).

## 공통 지시문
```
The attached images are monsters from my game. Draw ONE image containing THREE NEW monsters for a different country, in exactly the same pixel art style as the attached monsters (same detail level, same outline thickness, same shading). Only copy the art style, not the characters.

Layout: three monsters standing side by side in a single row, left to right: NORMAL monster A, NORMAL monster B, BOSS. At least 40 pixels of empty magenta space between neighbors so they can be cut apart. Same ground line: all feet touch the same bottom line.
Sizes: A and B are small cute field monsters (chubby chibi, oversized head, short limbs, big round eyes, a little fierce but friendly). The BOSS is clearly bigger (about 1.5x taller), more imposing and detailed, but in the same style.

Style rules:
- True pixel art on a strict square pixel grid, hard edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients.
- Dark purple-black 1-pixel outline, 2-3 tone cel shading, light from the top-left, rich saturated colors.
- All three face LEFT in side 3/4 view, full body, battle-ready pose.
- Arms, legs, tail and weapons slightly apart from the body.
- Background: one flat solid #FF00FF magenta. No ground, no shadow, no scenery, no text, no labels, no frame, no watermark.
- Design each monster from its own country's folklore and culture (not a copy of the Korean ones).

Country:
```
