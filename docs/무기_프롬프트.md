# 무기 그림 요청 프롬프트 (제미나이 등 그림 AI용)

첨부: 고양이·코기 그림(바탕화면 `몬스터_참고그림`) + 바꿀 무기의 지금 그림(바탕화면 `무기_참고그림`). 공통 지시문 끝 "Weapon:" 뒤에 한 줄. 한 번에 하나씩, 같은 무기의 등급 4장은 한 대화에서 이어서.

게임에서 무기를 쥐는 위치: 칼·지팡이·전설 칼/지팡이는 48칸 그림의 왼쪽 아래(손잡이)에서 오른쪽 위로 대각선, 활은 가운데 손잡이(세로), 석궁은 가로. 그래서 방향을 지금 그림과 똑같이 유지해야 함.

## 공통 지시문
```
The attached cat and corgi are my game's player characters and define the art style. The other attached image is the current weapon icon in my game.
Redraw this ONE weapon as a pixel art item in exactly the same art style as the cat and corgi.

Rules:
- Strict square pixel grid, 48x48 pixels (hard-edged upscale is fine), no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients.
- Thick dark purple-black 1-pixel outline, 2-3 tone cel shading, light from the top-left, rich saturated colors.
- Keep the SAME orientation and layout as the attached current weapon image (this is important, the game holds it at that point).
- The weapon alone: no hand, no character, no text, no frame, no shadow.
- Flat solid #FF00FF magenta background.
- Chunky, readable silhouette that stays clear when shown small.

Weapon:
```

## 한 번에 요청하기 (두 장으로 나눠서)
첨부: 고양이, 코기, `무기_참고그림/_전체_무기_한눈에.png`.

### 1장째: 일반 무기 12개 (4열 x 3줄)
```
The attached cat and corgi are my game's player characters and define the art style. The attached weapon chart shows my game's current weapons.
Create ONE pixel art sprite sheet of 12 weapons in exactly the same art style as the cat and corgi.

Layout (very important):
- A grid of 4 columns x 3 rows, all items the same size, evenly spaced, with clear magenta space between them. Nothing touches or overlaps.
- Row 1 = SWORDS, row 2 = BOWS, row 3 = STAFFS.
- Columns left to right = grade: common, uncommon, rare, epic. Higher grade = more ornate and glowing.
  Grade colors: common plain grey/iron, uncommon green accents, rare blue accents, epic purple-silver accents with glow.
- Swords and staffs are diagonal: grip/bottom end at the bottom-left, tip/orb at the top-right.
- Bows are vertical: string on the left, an arrow nocked pointing right, grip at the center.
- The four items in a row are the same weapon type and design family, only getting fancier.

Style:
- True pixel art, each item about 48x48 pixels on a strict square pixel grid (hard-edged upscale is fine), no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients.
- Thick dark purple-black 1-pixel outline, 2-3 tone cel shading, light from the top-left, rich saturated colors.
- Weapons only: no hands, no characters, no text, no labels, no frame, no shadows.
- Flat solid #FF00FF magenta background.
```

### 2장째: 전설 무기 10개 (5열 x 2줄)
```
The attached cat and corgi are my game's player characters and define the art style. The attached weapon chart shows my game's current weapons.
Create ONE pixel art sprite sheet of 10 LEGENDARY weapons in exactly the same art style as the cat and corgi. They should look clearly more special than normal weapons: gold details, gems, a soft glow and sparkles.

Layout (very important):
- A grid of 5 columns x 2 rows, all items the same size, evenly spaced, with clear magenta space between them. Nothing touches or overlaps.
- Order, left to right, top row then bottom row:
  1 Excalibur: glowing pale-blue blade, golden crossguard, blue jewel, purple grip.
  2 Green Dragon Crescent Blade (Guan Yu's glaive): long wooden pole, large teal crescent blade with a dragon motif, red tassel, gold rings.
  3 Kusanagi sword: straight pale-green Japanese blade, round golden tsuba, red wrapped grip, green jewel.
  4 Thuan Thien sword of the golden turtle: golden blade, green turtle-shell guard with a little turtle head.
  5 Jumong's horn bow: short Korean composite bow, deep red lacquer, bone-white tips, blue grip, arrow with red fletching.
  6 Robin Hood's longbow: tall straight yew longbow, green grip, gold coin charm, arrow with green fletching.
  7 William Tell's crossbow: wooden stock pointing right, steel bow arms, bolt loaded, small red apple charm (horizontal).
  8 Merlin's staff: twisted wooden staff with branches wrapping a glowing blue crystal, star sparkles.
  9 Queen Mother of the West's peach staff: golden staff topped with a pink immortal peach and green leaves.
  10 Anubis was-scepter: black staff with gold bands, black jackal head on top with gold details.
- Swords, the glaive and staffs are diagonal: grip/bottom end at the bottom-left, tip at the top-right.
- Bows are vertical: string on the left, arrow pointing right, grip at the center. The crossbow is horizontal, pointing right.

Style:
- True pixel art, each item about 48x48 pixels on a strict square pixel grid (hard-edged upscale is fine), no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients.
- Thick dark purple-black 1-pixel outline, 2-3 tone cel shading, light from the top-left, rich saturated colors.
- Weapons only: no hands, no characters, no text, no labels, no frame, no shadows.
- Flat solid #FF00FF magenta background.
```
